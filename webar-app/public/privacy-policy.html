Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

Memoera ("we", "our", "us") operates the Memoera website and mobile application (together, the "Service"), which lets users attach videos, 3D models, links, and other content to physical images so they can be viewed in augmented reality (AR) by scanning the image. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: first and last name, mobile number, optional email address, date of birth, and a security question — provided when you sign up.
  • Profile photo: if you choose to upload one.
  • Content you upload: images, videos, 3D models, documents, and links you attach to your AR targets, plus whether you mark them private or public.
  • Reviews: the rating and text you submit if you write a review.
  • Payment records: if you purchase a premium plan, payment is processed by Razorpay — we store the order/transaction reference, not your card or bank details.
  • Device/usage data: basic technical data (device type, app version, crash logs) used to keep the Service working reliably.

Camera Access

Memoera uses your device camera to scan AR target images and detect matches in real time. Camera video is processed live, on your device, to find a match — it is never recorded, stored, or transmitted to our servers or anyone else.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account, and let you sign in.
  • To store and serve the AR content you upload, and match it to the correct scanned image.
  • To process premium plan payments and referral bonuses.
  • To send account-related notifications (e.g. OTP codes, festival greetings) via SMS or in-app messages.
  • To improve reliability and troubleshoot issues.

Third-Party Services We Use

  • Cloudflare R2 — stores uploaded media (images, videos, 3D models, documents).
  • Neon (PostgreSQL) — stores account and app data.
  • Razorpay — processes premium plan payments.
  • 2Factor — sends SMS one-time passwords (OTP) for signup/verification.
  • Google AdMob — shows banner and rewarded video ads inside the Memoera mobile app.
  • Google AdSense — shows ads on the Memoera website (memoera.in).

Google AdMob and AdSense may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to show ads, including personalized ads based on your interests. You can review or opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers listed above, solely to operate the Service, and where required by law. Content you mark public is, by design, viewable by anyone who scans the associated image.

Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your account and content for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual AR targets at any time from the Gallery. To request full account deletion, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

Memoera is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
